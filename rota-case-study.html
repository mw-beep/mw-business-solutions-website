The Problem

Where the labour savings come from.

An inefficient rota can put more people on shift than needed at certain times of day. Those extra hours add to the labour bill. The tool finds a better fit between shifts and required cover, reducing that overstaffing while keeping the business’s rules in place.

The staffing rules the tool follows

It reduces overstaffing while respecting:

  • Staff availability and time off
  • Contracted hours and maximum working hours
  • The number of staff needed throughout the day
  • Keyholder cover, opening staff and fixed shifts

Local & offline

Built for the work laptop you already have.

The tool was installed and tested on the branch’s own work laptop. It runs entirely offline, so no cloud service is needed.

Adjustable for older laptops

The Cores setting controls how many processor cores the tool uses, and the time limit controls how long it searches for a rota. These can be adjusted to suit an older work laptop; using fewer cores may take longer. I’ll check performance on your machine during initial testing.
